Illusion of Support

cognitive extension

old tools are deterministic, AI tools are not. 
High level of uncertainties. How to manage that in high stakes/high risks environment. 
Increased Bloom level of understanding to effectively use AI:

Knowledge: Recall facts and basic concepts.
Comprehension: Explain ideas or concepts.
Application: Use information in new situations.
Analysis: Draw connections among ideas.
Synthesis: Combine parts to form a new whole.
Evaluation: Justify a stand or decision.


Are we skipping Analysis, Synthesis, and Evaluation?
